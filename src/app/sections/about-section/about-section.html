I work where product thinking, visual design, and engineering meet. I care about the full experience: how clearly an interface communicates, how naturally it behaves, and whether the system behind it remains reliable and maintainable as the product grows.

My background in frontend and mobile development and game design gives me both a practical technical foundation and a strong instinct for systems, feedback, and user experience. I can step back to shape a direction, then zoom in and build the details that make it work.

I’m at my best with teams and clients who need someone to take ownership, communicate clearly, and carry an idea from an early conversation to a finished product.
